# BringIt – Smart Online Grocery Delivery Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

**BringIt** is an online grocery delivery platform inspired by quick-commerce applications such as Blinkit. The platform allows customers to browse grocery products, place orders, and get them delivered to their doorstep.

The key feature that differentiates BringIt from traditional quick-commerce platforms is its **Trusted Local Store Integration**.

In addition to BringIt's own inventory, selected and trusted local general stores can register as partner stores on the platform. If a particular product is unavailable or out of stock in BringIt's inventory, the system can search for a suitable registered partner store that has the required product.

If the partner store is located along or near the delivery route, the delivery partner can collect the item from that store and complete the customer's order.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Quick-commerce platforms generally depend on their own warehouses or dark stores to fulfill customer orders. When a requested product is unavailable, customers may have to choose an alternative product or receive an incomplete order.

BringIt aims to reduce such situations by connecting the platform with **verified local general stores**.

The system can utilize the inventory of these trusted stores when BringIt's own inventory cannot fulfill a particular item, improving product availability and reducing order cancellations or incomplete deliveries.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

BringIt introduces a **Smart Partner Store Fulfillment** mechanism.

The basic workflow is:

1. The customer searches for and selects grocery products.
2. The customer places an order.
3. BringIt checks its own inventory for the requested products.
4. If all products are available, the order is fulfilled normally.
5. If a product is unavailable, the system searches registered partner stores.
6. The system identifies a suitable partner store based on product availability and its proximity to the delivery route.
7. The delivery partner collects the unavailable item from the selected partner store.
8. The complete order is delivered to the customer.

## 👥 Main Users

### Customer
- Browse and search for grocery products
- Add products to cart
- Place orders
- Track orders
- Receive doorstep delivery

### BringIt Admin
- Manage products and inventory
- Manage customers and delivery partners
- Verify and manage partner stores
- Monitor orders and fulfillment

### Partner Store
- Register with BringIt
- Maintain product availability
- Accept item pickup requests
- Provide required products to delivery partners

### Delivery Partner
- Receive delivery assignments
- Pick up orders
- Collect required items from partner stores when necessary
- Deliver orders to customers

## 🚀 Project Objective

The primary objective of BringIt is to create a reliable and efficient online grocery delivery system that can **reduce product unavailability by utilizing trusted local stores as an additional fulfillment network**.

The project also aims to demonstrate the application of software engineering principles, design principles, version control, system design, and collaborative software development.
